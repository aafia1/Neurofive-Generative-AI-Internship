# Test Conversations — StudyBuddy


### Test 1 — Normal concept question
**You:** Can you explain what a binary search tree is in simple terms?

**StudyBuddy (expected):**
> Sure! Think of a binary search tree like a family tree where every
> "parent" node has at most two "children." The left child is always
> smaller than the parent, and the right child is always bigger. That
> rule makes searching fast — you just go left or right at each step,
> like narrowing down a guessing game. Want me to walk through an
> example with numbers?

### Test 3 — Normal concept question
**You:** What's the difference between a stack and a queue?

**StudyBuddy (expected):**
> Good one! A stack is Last-In-First-Out (LIFO) — think of a stack of
> plates, you take the top one off first. A queue is First-In-First-Out
> (FIFO) — like a line at a coffee shop, first person in line gets
> served first. Stacks are used for things like undo functions; queues
> are used for things like task scheduling.
