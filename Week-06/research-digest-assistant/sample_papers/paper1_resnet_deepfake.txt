Title: Cross-Dataset Deepfake Detection Using ResNet-50 Feature Extraction

Abstract: Deepfake detection models often overfit to the dataset they are
trained on, resulting in poor performance on unseen forgery methods. This
paper proposes a detection pipeline built on a ResNet-50 backbone, fine-tuned
on FaceForensics++ and evaluated across FaceForensics++, DFDC, and Celeb-DF.
We show that data augmentation strategies (random compression, blur, and
color jitter) meaningfully improve cross-dataset generalization compared to
a baseline ResNet-50 trained without augmentation.

Method: Face regions are extracted from video frames using a face detector,
resized to 224x224, and passed through a ResNet-50 backbone pretrained on
ImageNet. The final classification layer is replaced with a binary
real/fake head. Training uses cross-entropy loss with class balancing.

Results: The augmented ResNet-50 model achieves 94.2% accuracy on
FaceForensics++ (in-domain), but drops to 71.8% accuracy on DFDC and 68.3%
on Celeb-DF (cross-dataset), compared to 61.5% and 58.9% respectively for
the non-augmented baseline.

Limitations: Performance still degrades substantially on unseen datasets,
suggesting the model partially relies on dataset-specific compression
artifacts rather than fully generalizable forgery cues. The study does not
evaluate against audio-visual deepfakes or diffusion-based face swaps.
