Title: U-Net and EfficientNetB4 for Satellite-Based Flood Segmentation

Abstract: Rapid flood mapping from satellite imagery is critical for
disaster response. This paper presents a U-Net segmentation model with an
EfficientNetB4 encoder for pixel-wise flood detection from Sentinel-1
SAR imagery, evaluated on a labeled flood segmentation dataset.

Method: A U-Net architecture with an EfficientNetB4 encoder pretrained on
ImageNet is trained to segment flooded vs non-flooded pixels. Training uses
a combined Dice and binary cross-entropy loss to handle class imbalance
between flooded and non-flooded regions.

Results: The model achieves a Dice score of 0.892 and IoU of 0.831 on the
held-out test set, outperforming a plain U-Net baseline (Dice 0.847, IoU
0.774) trained without the pretrained encoder.

Limitations: Performance drops in regions with dense urban structures,
where SAR backscatter patterns are harder to distinguish from flooded
surfaces. The model has not been validated on optical (non-SAR) imagery.
